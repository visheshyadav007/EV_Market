01 / Project Overview

Understanding the Electric Vehicle Revolution

The electric vehicle industry is transforming global transportation. This project uses market data to explore manufacturers, vehicle categories, battery technologies and customer preferences.

⚡

The Industry

Electric mobility is expanding through advancements in battery technology, sustainability initiatives and changing consumer demand.

📊

The Analysis

Explore sales performance, manufacturer comparisons, vehicle specifications and customer ratings through data visualization.

🌱

The Impact

Understand how electric mobility and battery innovations relate to the transition toward more sustainable transportation.

02 / Problem Statement

What Drives the EV Market?

The growing electric vehicle industry generates large volumes of market and operational data. Understanding sales performance, manufacturer competition, battery technologies, vehicle range and customer satisfaction requires a unified analytical approach. This project aims to transform such data into meaningful insights that support informed decisions.
